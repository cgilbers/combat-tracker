# Combat Tracker

The Combat Tracker helps a Dungeon Master prepare and run tabletop combat encounters. Its core language describes the party, the creatures and characters in an encounter, and their progress through combat.

## Language

**Adventuring Party**:
A roster of player characters the Dungeon Master can bring into an encounter.
_Avoid_: Team, group (when referring to the saved roster)

**Player Character (PC)**:
A player-controlled adventurer who belongs to an adventuring party and may take part in an encounter.
_Avoid_: Player (the person at the table), character (when the distinction matters)

**Creature**:
A non-player entity represented by combat statistics that can be included in an encounter.
_Avoid_: Monster (unless specifically referring to a monster in source material)

**Encounter**:
A prepared or active combat scenario containing combatants and their combat order.
_Avoid_: Battle (when referring to the saved scenario)

**Combatant**:
A player character or creature participating in an encounter.
_Avoid_: Unit

**Initiative**:
A combatant's relative position in the turn order, usually determined at the start of an encounter.
_Avoid_: Turn order (the full ordered list)

**Initiative Group**:
A set of combatants that share one initiative result.
_Avoid_: Group (when referring to a saved collection of combatants)

**Turn**:
One combatant's opportunity to act during a round.
_Avoid_: Move

**Round**:
A cycle in which each eligible combatant in an encounter has an opportunity to take a turn.
_Avoid_: Turn (an individual combatant's opportunity)

**Delayed Combatant**:
A combatant that has temporarily left the active turn order and may re-enter it under the encounter's delay rules.
_Avoid_: Skipped combatant

**Boss Resources**:
Limited-use abilities tracked for a creature, such as spell slots, legendary actions, legendary resistances, or lair actions.
_Avoid_: Boss stats

**SRD Creature**:
A creature based on content from the System Reference Document (SRD).
_Avoid_: Official creature (which may imply content outside the SRD)

**Homebrew Creature**:
A user-created creature that is not sourced from the SRD.
_Avoid_: Custom monster (use Creature when source distinction is unnecessary)

**Encounter Environment**:
A package of setting details associated with an encounter, such as terrain, hazards, interactive elements, and dynamic events.
_Avoid_: Map (unless referring specifically to a map layout)
